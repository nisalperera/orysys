# Product spec: Meridian Wallet
## Summary
Retail wallet with top-up, P2P transfer and merchant QR payments. Daily transfer limit is LKR 200,000 for verified users.
## Non-functional requirements
Audit logging, PII masking in logs, and PCI DSS scope review.
