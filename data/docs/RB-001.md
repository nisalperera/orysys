# Runbook: payments-api connection pool saturation
## Detection
Check pool saturation dashboard.
## Mitigation steps
1. Acknowledge the alert and open an incident channel.
2. Run `SELECT count(*) FROM pg_stat_activity` and kill reconciliation sessions older than 60s, then scale payments-api replicas by +2.
3. Validate recovery using the payment success rate dashboard (target above 99.5%).
## Escalation
Escalate to the platform lead after 15 minutes without recovery.
