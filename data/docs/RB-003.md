# Runbook: NovaPay gateway degradation
## Detection
Check NovaPay latency panel and vendor status page.
## Mitigation steps
1. Acknowledge the alert and open an incident channel.
2. Open the circuit breaker manually, route settlements to the fallback queue, page the vendor escalation contact.
3. Validate recovery using the payment success rate dashboard (target above 99.5%).
## Escalation
Escalate to the platform lead after 15 minutes without recovery.
