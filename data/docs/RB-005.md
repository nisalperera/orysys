# Runbook: major incident communication
## Detection
Declare severity and open incident channel.
## Mitigation steps
1. Acknowledge the alert and open an incident channel.
2. Post status update every 30 minutes, notify the regulator liaison for P1 events lasting over 60 minutes.
3. Validate recovery using the payment success rate dashboard (target above 99.5%).
## Escalation
Escalate to the platform lead after 15 minutes without recovery.
