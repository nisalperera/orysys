# Runbook: card-switch TLS certificate renewal
## Detection
Verify certificate expiry in inventory.
## Mitigation steps
1. Acknowledge the alert and open an incident channel.
2. Request new certificate from PKI team, deploy to card-switch-gw via the cert rotation pipeline, verify handshake with the card network test endpoint.
3. Validate recovery using the payment success rate dashboard (target above 99.5%).
## Escalation
Escalate to the platform lead after 15 minutes without recovery.
