# Product spec: Meridian Visa Platinum Card
## Summary
Contactless card with 3D Secure, travel insurance, and a monthly statement cycle. Annual fee LKR 12,000, waived above LKR 1.5M annual spend.
## Non-functional requirements
Audit logging, PII masking in logs, and PCI DSS scope review.
