# Runbook: emergency database failover (admin only)
## Detection
Obtain two admin approvals.
## Mitigation steps
1. Acknowledge the alert and open an incident channel.
2. Promote the standby replica, update the writer endpoint, validate with synthetic transactions.
3. Validate recovery using the payment success rate dashboard (target above 99.5%).
## Escalation
Escalate to the platform lead after 15 minutes without recovery.
